About

📖 About KnowGen AI

KnowGen AI is an Executive Intelligent Knowledge Assistant developed using Python, Streamlit, LangChain, Groq AI, and FAISS. It enables seamless multi-document understanding, voice querying, flashcard generation, and automated PDF note synthesis.
